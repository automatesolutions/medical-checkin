How to use Medical Check-In & Admin

One place for wildfire Medical Unit staff to see who checked in, who is assigned, and who is demobilizing — without Excel copying or clinical data. Use this page as the day-to-day how-to.

01

Start here — the simple picture

Public check-in (phones)

Field people open a link or scan a QR and submit one form per person. No Google login. No file uploads.

Admin (Medical Unit)

Staff open the admin site to manage the roster, Glide Path, review queue, document statuses, and print the QR.

The QR code is not on the public form. Print it from Admin → Check-in form. Scanning that QR opens the public form.
02

04

Field check-in (public form)

Each person submits once for themselves. Every ambulance and REMS member submits a separate response.

1
Open the formScan the incident QR, or open the public link above. You should see the incident name (e.g. Bearclaw Creek).
2
Step 1 — Order & identityResource Order # (O or E), email, first name, last name.
3
Step 2 — Role & vehiclePosition, company, vehicle type / call sign as prompted.
4
Step 3 — Assignment datesDate assigned and length of assignment (drives Glide Path).
5
Step 4 — ExperienceSelect experience options that apply.
6
Step 5 — CapabilitiesSelect capabilities / certifications that apply.
7
Step 6 — Documents checklistMark what you will email. Do not upload files in the form.
8
SubmitConfirmation shows a response id and the Fire email address for mailing Contract, DL, NRCMT, State license, and Medical Director letter (if Narcotics).
Nothing clinical or patient-related is collected. Keep documents in email — the app only tracks status.
05

Where is the QR?

  1. Open Admin.
  2. In the left nav, open Check-in form.
  3. Print or screenshot the QR for posting at ICP / briefing.

The QR encodes only the public form URL for this incident. Respondents never sign into Admin.

06

Admin — daily workflow

1
Open AdminConfirm the correct incident is selected (e.g. Bearclaw Creek). Keep the tab open — it shows Live and refreshes about every 8 seconds.
2
Print / share the QRCheck-in form page → post for field use.
3
Watch RosterNew check-ins appear automatically with a banner (and browser notification if you allow it). Ember-bordered fields are the only admin edits.
4
Clear Review queueIf a person couldn’t be safely matched, decide: link to a candidate resource or create provisional.
5
Update Glide Path viewPrint landscape for briefings. Watch Red / LWD / DMB.
6
Cycle document statusesAs Fire email receives packets, update status only — no files in the app.
07

Roster

One row per person, grouped under the resource they were ordered on.

  • Filters: All · Active · Enroute · Needs Review
  • Click a person to open the detail drawer.
  • Ember-marked fields (admin-editable): Division, Camp, effective dates, extension, status — submitted form answers stay locked.
  • Counters at the top show Resources, Personnel, Enroute, LWD/DMB, Review.
08

Glide Path

14-day rolling view of workdays remaining, grouped by resource.

  • Last work day (LWD) = first work day + length − 1 + extension days
  • DMB / Travel starts the day after LWD
  • Colors: Green · Yellow · Red · LWD · DMB/TVL · REVIEW
Print for the trailer / ICP board

1. Open Admin → Glide Path
2. Click Print / Save PDF
3. In the system dialog: set orientation to Landscape
4. Choose Save as PDF, or send to the large-format / plotter printer
5. If colors look washed out, enable Background graphics (Chrome) or equivalent

09

Review queue

Shown when the system cannot safely decide which resource a person belongs to. Nothing auto-merges from here.

  • Link the person to a suggested / existing resource, or
  • Create a provisional resource instance

Every decision is audited. Keep this queue at zero when you can.

10

Documents

Status tracker only. Files live in the Fire email (shown on the page).

Cycle status:

Not Required → Requested → Received → Verified → Rejected → Expired

If the form selected Narcotics, track the Medical Director letter status as well.

11

Medical Plan (ICS 206)

Inside Admin → Medical Plan. This is an ICS 206 / 206 WF–style worksheet for the IAP and print trailer — not a public form.

  1. Open Medical Plan in the left nav.
  2. Fill ambulances, air ambulance, hospitals, aid-station capability, and emergency procedures.
  3. Use the roster hint list (from check-ins) as a reminder of who is on scene.
  4. Click Save, then Print / Save PDF (landscape, background graphics on).
  5. Have SOF review / sign per incident SOPs — the app stores the draft and printout aids the packet.
12

Close incident / start clean

  • Close the incident when check-in should stop (no new public submissions).
  • Create a clean incident from Admin → Check-in form when reusing the system — it copies no people, responses, or ids.
13

Do / don’t

Do

· One submission per person
· Print QR from Admin
· Email docs to the Fire address
· Edit only ember admin fields
· Clear Review before briefings

Don’t

· Upload files into the form
· Put QR on the public form page
· Store clinical / patient data
· Expect Admin and Check-in to be the same URL
· Copy rows from Excel into this app
